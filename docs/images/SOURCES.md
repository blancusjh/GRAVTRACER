# Image sources

`python/grayt/assets/nasa_starmap_2020_4k.jpg` is a display JPEG derived from
the 4K OpenEXR celestial map in [NASA Goddard Scientific Visualization Studio's
Deep Star Maps 2020](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/4851/) (visualization by Ernie
Wright). The map draws positions, brightness, and colors from star catalogs.
For the bundled JPEG, the linear EXR was raised by two exposure stops and
mapped to display values with FFmpeg's Mobius tonemap (`param=0.3`,
`desat=0`); positions were not changed. Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight
Center Scientific Visualization Studio. NASA SVS says its visualizations are
[public domain unless otherwise noted](https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/).
The bundled loader reverses the source map's horizontal axis to match
GRAVTRACER's longitude convention.

`kerr_disk.png`, `ray_trajectories_3d.png`, and `image_formation_scene.png`
are generated by `examples/readme_figures.py` from the project's rendering
code, the bundled NASA sky map, and the engraving in `examples/assets/`. `model_gallery.png` is generated by
`examples/model_gallery.py`.
Disk colors are display mappings. The Kerr rays use a pseudo-Cartesian
coordinate embedding.

`../animations/kerr_orbit.mp4` and `kerr_orbit.gif` are rendered by
`examples/kerr_movie.py`. The GIF is the MP4 played 1.5 times slower.
